### CPQ Key Features

1) **Favoriting Products** - Mark a product as a favorite to allow you to quickly add them to future quotes  
2) **Quote Line Groups** - Allows you to group products together and apply parameters to just that group of products instead of the whole quote  
3) **Twin Fields** - This allows you to do automatic data mapping between two fields on two different objects  
4) **Product Rules** - Rules you can setup that prevent invalid product combinations  
5) **Price Rules** - Rules you can setup to automatically adjust the price of an product (for instance a discount price rule)  
6) **Guided Selling** - A configurable interface that allows you to show sales reps the best product to sell a customer based on their inputs.  
7) **Summary Variables** - Similar to rollups, but only present themselves in the quote line editor and do not contribute to roll up summary limits (25 per obj - 40 max).  
8) **Contract Amendments and Renewals** - Automated functionality to allow you to create amendments to contracts, as well as auto-renew a contract.   
9) **Contracted Pricing** - Allows you to setup contracted price discounts for products on an account by account basis.  
10) Multi-Dimensional Quoting - Allows you to allow for discount/price to vary for a product over the lifespan of a contract. Allows you to mix subscription based products with non-subscription based products in a quote.
