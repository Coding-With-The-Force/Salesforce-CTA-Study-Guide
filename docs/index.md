### What is this wiki?

THIS IS CURRENTLY STILL VERY MUCH A WORK IN PROGRESS AND WILL BE FOR MANY MONTHS!

This wiki houses all my notes for the CTA Exam as well as practice scenarios I'm legally allowed to share, example diagrams, etc. Much of the content in this wiki are the most important pieces of different CTA guides available out there in the world. 
When studying for this exam consider additionally reading through and studying the following:  
* [Becoming a Salesforce Certified Technical Architect](https://amzn.to/3iHGIBz)  
* Architect Exam Trailmixes
* [The Architect Trailblazers Group](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/trailblazer-community/groups/0F930000000blKv?tab=discussion&sort=LAST_MODIFIED_DATE_DESC)  

_**DISCLAIMER:**_ While this wiki hopes to simplify and make studying and preparing for the exam easier and less challenging to figure out, I do not believe it is possible to pass this exam without the help of many other people. You need to form a study group of 3-4 people to study this content with, but more specifically to solve and run CTA scenarios together. You NEED other strong architects in your group, not just your friends or close work colleagues. They must challenge you and be ok with you challenging them in the QA portion of every scenario you solve. Their alternative perspectives will help you hone and improve your own skills. You should also have an existing CTA coach you if possible as it will definitely increase your chances of being prepared and passing. You can find these people in the Architect Trailblazers Group, flowrepublic or in consultancy firms internal CTA training programs.
