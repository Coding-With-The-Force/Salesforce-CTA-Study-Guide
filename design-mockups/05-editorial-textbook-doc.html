CHAPTER 8 · PART III — SECURITY

OAuth Flows & Identity and Access Management

Identity and Access Management comes up in virtually every CTA scenario. This chapter walks through the terms the judges expect you to know, the standards you will lean on, and every OAuth 2.0 flow you might be asked to justify on the board — with the trade-offs that make each one the right or wrong answer.

What is IAM?

Identity and Access Management is the framework that verifies who a user is — authentication — and what they are permitted to do — authorization — across every system in your landscape, Salesforce included.

Key IAM Terms

Identity Provider (IdP)Stores identities and authenticates the user — Okta, Entra ID, or Salesforce itself.
Service Provider (SP)The application the user wants to access; it trusts the IdP's word for who they are.
PrincipalThe user or system whose identity is being verified.
