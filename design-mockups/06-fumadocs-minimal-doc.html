OAuth Flows & Identity and Access Management

IAM comes up in virtually every CTA scenario. This guide covers the terms the judges expect you to know, the standards you will lean on, and every OAuth 2.0 flow you might need to justify on the board.

What is IAM?

Identity and Access Management is the framework that verifies who a user is (authentication) and what they are allowed to do (authorization) across every system in your landscape.

Key IAM Terms

TermWhat it means
Identity Provider (IdP)Stores identities and authenticates the user — Okta, Entra ID, or Salesforce itself.
Service Provider (SP)The app the user wants to access; it trusts the IdP's word for who they are.
PrincipalThe user or system whose identity is being verified.
Board tip: when a scenario says "users must not re-enter credentials", say the standard (SAML or OIDC), the flow, and who acts as IdP — all three, every time.
