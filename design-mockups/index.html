CTA Exam Guide — 10 Redesign Directions

Each direction has two mockups: the redesigned homepage and a matching doc/reading page (using the real nav sections and the OAuth Flows & IAM guide as the sample article). Open any file directly in your browser — they are fully self-contained.

01 · Trailhead Modern

Light, friendly, Salesforce-blue. Instantly familiar to your Trailhead audience. Tradeoff: the safest option — least differentiated.

HomepageDoc page

02 · Dark Command Center

Dark-first with glowing blue accents — honors your current site's dark default. Tradeoff: dark UIs make long-form reading harder for some.

HomepageDoc page

03 · Stripe-style Docs

Clean white, refined typography, quiet gradient accents. Reads as premium and trustworthy. Tradeoff: personality comes from polish, not flair.

HomepageDoc page

04 · Linear App-style

Near-black, dense, keyboard-first. Feels like a fast tool, not a website. Tradeoff: density can read as cold for a study guide.

HomepageDoc page

05 · Editorial Textbook

Serif, cream paper, chapters and figures — a premium printed study guide on the web. Tradeoff: least "modern React app" of the ten.

HomepageDoc page

06 · Fumadocs Minimal

The modern shadcn-era docs default: neutral, hairline borders, zero noise. Easiest to build. Tradeoff: looks like many other modern docs sites.

HomepageDoc page

07 · Bold Gradient Launch

Loud hero, hard shadows, big claims — a marketing-grade front door for the guide and coaching. Tradeoff: boldness can clash with long study sessions.

HomepageDoc page

08 · Architect's Blueprint

Blueprint grid, title blocks, sheet numbers — leans into the diagram-first identity of the CTA exam. Tradeoff: must be executed carefully or it reads as a gimmick.

HomepageDoc page

09 · Learning Platform

An LMS-style dashboard: per-domain progress, a study plan, "continue where you left off". Strongest returning-visitor experience. Tradeoff: most build effort.

DashboardDoc page

10 · Bento Dashboard

A bento grid that shows all eight domains at a glance, sized by weight. Modern and scannable. Tradeoff: the grid is homepage-only flair; inner pages go conventional.

Blueprint Series — 10 variations on direction 08

Ten homepage riffs on the Architect's Blueprint direction. Same DNA — grid paper, mono labels, title blocks, sheet numbers — with different paper, ink, and how literal the drafting metaphor gets.

11 · Whiteprint

The original inverted: white drafting paper with blue ink. Same layout as direction 08, far easier on the eyes for long reading.

12 · Ink & Vellum

Cream paper, black ink, a red rotated "FREE · $0" stamp, and the domains as a numbered drawing list. Classic engineering blackline.

13 · Engineer's Pad

Green graph-paper notebook with a "worked example" card in the hero — leans into showing your work, step by step.

14 · Deep Cyanotype

Darker, moodier take on the original with glowing cyan linework and a real system-landscape diagram drawn right in the hero.

15 · Sepia Draft

Aged paper and brown ink — "plans of record" from the archives, with folios and Roman numerals. The warmest of the set.

16 · Isometric Works

Light isometric grid with an exploded solution-stack drawing in the hero — the domains as layers that fit together.

Homepage

17 · Redline Review

A white sheet returned with red review comments and handwritten margin notes — "get redlined here, not at your board." The most storytelling of the ten.

Homepage

18 · Modern Hybrid

A clean modern site where the blueprint lives inside the hero panel and the mono labels carry the theme. The most practical to build and the safest to read.

Merge Studies — Engineer's Pad × Schematic

Three ways to combine direction 13's green graph-paper notebook with direction 20's domain-wiring schematic — from "pad absorbs the schematic" to "schematic pinned onto the pad" to a dark late-night blend.

21 · Schematic on the Pad

The Engineer's Pad absorbs the schematic: the full domain-wiring diagram redrawn in green ink on graph paper, with the §-numbered domain list and a worked-example card alongside. One material, one color, calmest of the three.

Homepage

22 · Two-Tone Bench

Both identities kept intact: the pad is the page, and the schematic stays a navy plotted print taped up beside your notes. The two-material contrast makes the diagram the hero. Slightly busier, most memorable.

Homepage

23 · Night Bench

The dark blend: the pad's green becomes a phosphor glow on a near-black bench — graph grid, glowing schematic, and the worked example side by side. Honors your current site's dark default; hardest for long reading.
